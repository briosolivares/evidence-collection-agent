# YC Winter 2024 AI Companies — Selection Summary

Source: Y Combinator Startup Directory (https://www.ycombinator.com/companies), filtered to
Batch = Winter 2024. Five companies were selected that carry the "Artificial Intelligence"
industry tag on their official YC company pages.

## Companies Selected

1. **Marr Labs** — AI-voice agents that are indistinguishable from humans.
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/marr-labs
   Founders: Dave Grannan (Co-founder & CEO), Han Shu (Co-founder & CTO)

2. **RetailReady** — An AI-powered supply chain compliance engine.
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retailready
   Founders: Elle Smyth (Co-founder), Sarah Hamer (Co-founder)

3. **Dragoneye** — Custom video detection models in less than 5 minutes.
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dragoneye
   Founders: Alex Liao (Founder)

4. **GetCrux** — AI Creative Strategist.
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/getcrux
   Founders: Himank Jain (Co-Founder & CEO), Atharva Padhye (Founder), Prabhat Singh (Founder)

5. **Silogy** — An AI-powered test and debug platform for chip developers.
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/silogy
   Founders: Kay Li (Founder/CEO), Paul Kim (Founder/CTO)

Total founders across all 5 companies: 10.

## Deliverable

`yc_w24_ai_founders_outreach.csv` contains one row per founder with columns:
`founder_name, linkedin_url, cold_outreach_email`. Each email is personalized using that
founder's specific background (prior companies, roles, education) and their company's
specific product, and each closes with a request for a 15-minute call.

LinkedIn URLs were taken directly from each founder's profile link listed on their
company's official YC page.
