# Pi Beta Phi (MIT - Massachusetts Gamma chapter)
Official site: https://mit.pibetaphi.org/
(Found via https://www.mitpanhel.org/pi-beta-phi -> official MIT Panhellenic site)

This official chapter site does NOT have a dedicated "Sisters"/members roster
page broken out by class year (unlike Alpha Phi, Alpha Chi Omega, Kappa Alpha
Theta, Sigma Kappa, Delta Phi Epsilon). It only has: Home, About, Recruitment,
Alumnae, Caregivers, Calendar, Leaders.

The "Leaders" page lists only officers (no class year or major):
- Teresa Picconatto - Chapter President
- Lexcea King - Vice President Operations
- Ariel Skolnick - Vice President Risk Management
- Sophia Song - Vice President Member Experience
- Meyline Szczepanski - Vice President Finance/Housing
- Lillian Poag - Vice President Recruitment
- Katelyn Gan - Director Recruitment Events
- Trang Phan - Director Membership Selection

No class year (senior/junior) is specified for these officers on the site,
and no general member roster with class of 2026 or class of 2027 breakdowns
is publicly available on the official Pi Beta Phi MIT website.
