# YC Winter 2024 AI-Focused Companies Selected

Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch=Winter%202024 (filtered/searched for AI, batch = Winter 2024)

All five companies below are tagged "Artificial Intelligence" / "AI" on their YC company pages and are part of the Winter 2024 (W24) batch.

1. **Retell AI** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retell-ai
   AI phone agents / voice AI for contact centers.
   Founders: Bing Wu (CEO), Todd Li (President), Evie Wang (CMO), Weijia Yu, Zexia Zhang (CTO)

2. **Greptile** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greptile
   AI code review agent with full codebase context.
   Founders: Daksh Gupta, Soohoon Choi (CTO), Vaishant Kameswaran

3. **Artisan** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/artisan
   AI BDR ("Ava") and AI sales team platform.
   Founder: Jaspar Carmichael-Jack

4. **Zep AI** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zep-ai
   Memory/context layer for AI agents (Graphiti knowledge graph library).
   Founder: Daniel Chalef

5. **Decipher AI** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/decipher-ai
   AI QA agents that generate and maintain E2E tests, plus vision-AI bug detection.
   Founders: Michael Rosenfield, Rohan Das
