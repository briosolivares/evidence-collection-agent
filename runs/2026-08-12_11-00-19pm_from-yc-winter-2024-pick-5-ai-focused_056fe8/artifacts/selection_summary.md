# YC Winter 2024 AI Companies — Selection Summary

Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch=Winter%202024 (YC Startup Directory)

Five AI-focused companies selected from the YC Winter 2024 batch (all explicitly tagged
"Artificial Intelligence" or closely adjacent AI/ML/Computer Vision tags on their YC company
pages), with founder count per company:

1. **Marr Labs** — AI-voice agents that are indistinguishable from humans.
   Tags: Artificial Intelligence, Conversational AI, AI Assistant
   Founders (2): Dave Grannan, Han Shu

2. **RetailReady** — An AI-powered supply chain compliance engine.
   Tags: B2B, Compliance, Logistics, Supply Chain, AI
   Founders (2): Elle Smyth, Sarah Hamer

3. **GetCrux** — AI Creative Strategist.
   Tags: Artificial Intelligence, SaaS, Analytics, Marketing, Automation
   Founders (3): Himank Jain, Atharva Padhye, Prabhat Singh

4. **kater.ai** — Deliver complete data analysis from a single question.
   Tags: Artificial Intelligence, Analytics, Data Engineering
   Founders (2): Yvonne Chou, Robin Seitz

5. **Dragoneye** — Custom video detection models in less than 5 minutes.
   Tags: Developer Tools, Computer Vision, AI, ML
   Founders (1): Alex Liao (only active founder listed on the YC company page)

Total founder rows in the CSV: 11.
