# Notes on data collection

Source: https://x.com/elonmusk (accessed logged-out)

X.com only renders a limited number of posts (5) to unauthenticated
visitors before covering the feed with a "See Elon Musk's full profile /
Log in or sign up" wall (see elonmusk_profile_view.png). No login
credentials were available in this environment, so the CSV reflects all
tweets that were accessible on the public profile page at the time of
capture.

All 5 posts retrieved show relative timestamps of 8h or less (1h, 5h, 5h,
6h, 8h), consistent with all being posted earlier today (no absolute date
stamps, which X only shows for posts older than the current day). These
are the only Elon Musk posts observable without authentication; if he
posted additional tweets today beyond what the logged-out view exposes,
they are not captured here due to the platform's access restriction.
