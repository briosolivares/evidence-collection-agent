# YC Winter 2024 — AI-Focused Company Selection Notes

Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch=Winter%202024 (YC Startup Directory)

## Selected companies (5), all tagged "Artificial Intelligence" / "AI" on YC and Winter 2024 batch:

1. **Marr Labs** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/marr-labs
   - AI-voice agents that are indistinguishable from humans.
   - Industry tags: Artificial Intelligence, Conversational AI, AI Assistant
   - Founders: Dave Grannan (CEO), Han Shu (CTO)

2. **RetailReady** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retailready
   - An AI-powered supply chain compliance engine
   - Industry tags: B2B, Compliance, Logistics, Supply Chain, AI
   - Founders: Elle Smyth, Sarah Hamer

3. **Dragoneye** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dragoneye
   - Custom video detection models in less than 5 minutes
   - Industry tags: Developer Tools, Computer Vision, AI, ML
   - Founder: Alex Liao (only active founder listed on YC profile; team size 2)

4. **Silogy** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/silogy
   - An AI-powered test and debug platform for chip developers
   - Industry tags: Artificial Intelligence, Developer Tools, Semiconductors
   - Founders: Kay Li (CEO), Paul Kim (CTO)

5. **Reprompt** — https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reprompt
   - AI Agents for Location
   - Description explicitly built around "AI Agents"
   - Founders: Lukas Martinelli, Rob Balian
