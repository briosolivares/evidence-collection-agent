## Summary of Content
On January 29, 2026, Apple Inc. filed this Form 8-K to report that it issued a press
release announcing its financial results for its first fiscal quarter ended
December 27, 2025 (Q1 FY2026). The press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1. The
filing was signed by Kevan Parekh, Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer
of Apple Inc.
