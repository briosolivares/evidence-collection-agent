## Overall Summary

**Price range:** Across the 30 listings reviewed, total 7-night prices (as displayed, after weekly discounts where applied) ranged from about **$886** (a 1-bedroom apartment in Stateline) to about **$3,179** (a modern 3-bedroom cabin with a hot tub in Carnelian Bay). The average total price across all 30 listings was roughly **$1,870** for the week (~$267/night average), though six listings' prices reflect fewer than 7 nights (see note below) so are not directly comparable on a per-week basis without adjustment.

**Property types:** The mix skewed heavily toward **cabins** (14 of 30), followed by houses/homes (7), condos (4), apartments (3), and chalets (2) — consistent with Tahoe's rustic mountain-lodging character.

**Location trends:** Listings clustered around the lake's north and west shores. **Tahoe City** was the most common location (10 listings), followed by Truckee (4), South Lake Tahoe (3), Carnelian Bay (3), Stateline (2), Kings Beach (2), Glenbrook (2), and Homewood (2), with one each in Incline Village and Zephyr Cove. This suggests Airbnb's algorithm favored the north/west shore area (Tahoe City, Truckee, Carnelian Bay) for this search, with fewer results from the south shore casino/ski corridor (Stateline, South Lake Tahoe).

**Quality signals:** Ratings were very strong overall — the average rating across listings with reviews was about **4.89/5**, and more than half (16 of 30) carried Airbnb's "Guest Favorite" badge, with another 7 hosted by "Superhost"s. This indicates the search results page was populated mostly with well-established, highly-rated listings rather than new or unproven ones.

**Capacity and amenities:** Bedroom counts ranged from studio/1-bedroom cabins and condos (sleeping 2) up to a 5-bedroom home sleeping 7+ people. Common recurring amenities mentioned in listing titles/summaries included fireplaces, hot tubs, decks, lake or mountain views, proximity to ski resorts, and beach or lake access — reflecting Tahoe's dual identity as both a winter ski destination and a summer lake destination.

**Availability caveat:** Six of the 30 listings (roughly 1 in 5) were **not actually bookable for the full requested Aug 24–31 week** — Airbnb displayed them anyway with an alternate, shorter available date range (e.g., Aug 24–28, Aug 25–30, Aug 26–30, or Aug 27–Sep 2) because they had partial-week availability. This is typical Airbnb search behavior (showing "similar" alternate-date options to fill out result pages) and means a prospective traveler would need to adjust dates or look elsewhere for those specific six properties if a strict 7-night, Aug 24–31 stay is required.

**Conclusion:** For a 7-night stay in Lake Tahoe the week of Aug 24–31, 2026, travelers have a wide range of options — from budget-friendly studio/1-bedroom apartments and condos under $1,000–1,500 for the week, to mid-range 2–3 bedroom cabins with hot tubs and fireplaces in the $1,500–2,500 range, up to larger luxury homes near $3,000+ for groups of 6 or more. The North/West Shore (Tahoe City, Truckee, Carnelian Bay) offers the greatest concentration and variety of choices, and the overall quality bar (ratings, Guest Favorite/Superhost status) is high across the board.
