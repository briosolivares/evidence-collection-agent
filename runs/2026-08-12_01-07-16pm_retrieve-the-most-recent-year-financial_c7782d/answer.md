## Key facts confirmed from the source pages (Aritzia Q4/Fiscal 2026 press release, May 7, 2026)

- Fiscal 2026 = 52-week period ended **March 1, 2026**.
- Q4 2026 = 13-week period ended March 1, 2026.
- Fiscal 2026 net revenue increased 35.2% to **$3.70 billion**, compared to $2.74 billion in Fiscal 2025.
- Q4 2026 net revenue increased 32.6% to **$1.19 billion**, compared to $895.1 million in Q4 2025.
- Fiscal 2026 gross profit margin: 44.9%; SG&A as % of net revenue: 29.1%.
- Fiscal 2026 capital cash expenditures were $237.5 million (vs. $253.5 million in Fiscal 2025).
- During Fiscal 2026, the Company repurchased 1,371,109 SVS under the 2025 NCIB for $144.9 million.
- The Fiscal 2026 MD&A and Fiscal 2026 AIF are both dated May 7, 2026.

## Note on file downloads

This environment's browser renders PDFs through a built-in Chrome PDF-viewer extension. When the
`download` tool is used against any PDF URL in this environment (tested against the Aritzia CDN
files above and also against an unrelated third-party test PDF), it captures only the tiny (536-byte)
PDF-viewer host-page HTML wrapper rather than the underlying PDF bytes — this is a systemic tool/
environment limitation, not a problem with Aritzia's documents. The actual PDFs were confirmed to
render correctly and fully in the browser (screenshots below, hundreds of KB of rendered content),
proving the source links are valid and the documents are genuine, current, and accessible.

Because raw PDF bytes could not be captured as files in this run, this answer instead provides:
- The verified, current, direct source URLs for all three documents (table above), suitable for
  downloading directly with a standard browser or file download tool outside this environment.
- Screenshot evidence (below) showing each document's cover/first page as rendered from the exact
  source URL, and the Financial Reports page listing showing the links.
