# Selection Notes — YC Winter 2024 AI-Focused Companies

Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies (filtered to Batch = Winter 2024), individual company
profile pages under https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/<slug>.

## 5 companies selected (all tagged "Artificial Intelligence" or explicitly AI-branded on YC)

| Company | One-line description | Founders |
|---|---|---|
| Marr Labs | AI-voice agents that are indistinguishable from humans (for sales, support, scheduling calls) | Dave Grannan (CEO), Han Shu (CTO) |
| RetailReady | An AI-powered supply chain compliance engine (helps warehouses avoid retailer compliance chargebacks) | Elle Smyth (CEO), Sarah Hamer (CTO) |
| Reprompt | AI Agents for Location (location-data agents used by clients like Foursquare, TomTom) | Lukas Martinelli, Rob Balian |
| GetCrux | AI Creative Strategist to launch winning ads (analyzes ad creative patterns for performance marketers) | Himank Jain (CEO), Atharva Padhye, Prabhat Singh |
| kater.ai | AI that delivers complete data analysis from a single question (self-serve analytics agent "Butler") | Yvonne Chou (CEO), Robin Seitz (CTO) |

All 5 companies are from the YC Winter 2024 batch, based in San Francisco, and are explicitly AI product
companies per their YC "Artificial Intelligence" industry tag and/or company tagline.

Total founders across the 5 companies: 11 (one CSV row each).
