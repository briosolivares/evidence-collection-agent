Plan for CSV columns: name, class, major, affiliation, interests, other

- affiliation = sorority name
- class = "2026" or "2027" (Senior/Junior indicated in class field as e.g. "2026 (Senior)")
- major = MIT course number/major if given
- interests = activities/clubs/sports (this is the closest match to "interests")
- other = hometown, big/little, or any other leftover info

Data sources:
1. Alpha Chi Omega (axo.mit.edu/sisters/) - Class of 2027 only (names only, no other info). No 2026 present.
2. Alpha Phi (aphi.mit.edu/sisterhood/2026, /2027) - 2026 names only; 2027 has hometown+major
3. Delta Phi Epsilon (dphie.mit.edu/sisterhood/meet-the-sisters/) - 2026 (name+major), 2027 (name+major) - only 3 juniors listed
4. Kappa Alpha Theta (theta.mit.edu/2026s, /2027s) - full data: hometown, major, big/little, activities
5. Pi Beta Phi (mit.pibetaphi.org) - no class-year sister roster found; skip listing individual members (data not available)
6. Sigma Kappa (sigmakappa.mit.edu/class-of-2026, /class-of-2027) - full data: hometown, major, activities
