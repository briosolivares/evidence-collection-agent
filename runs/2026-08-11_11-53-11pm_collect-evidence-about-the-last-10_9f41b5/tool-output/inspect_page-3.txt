                    - heading "What Problem This Solves" [level=2] [ref=f9e245]
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e246]: Fixes Discord voice races where leaving during a pending join could reconnect later, a stopped session could subscribe after authorization, decoded STT work could outlive leave, followed-user state could resurrect an older move, realtime connect could report ready after close, and provider-reset completions could leak into a newer voice generation.
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e247]: The previous voice implementation also concentrated connection, follow, receive, realtime, playback, turn, and consult ownership in two files over 4,000 lines, making these lifecycle invariants difficult to express and verify.
                    - heading "Why This Change Was Made" [level=2] [ref=f9e248]
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e249]: The voice subsystem is split by lifecycle owner into runtime, guild session, following, receive, realtime session, playback, turns, and consult modules. One per-guild generation now authorizes connection publication, capture, realtime state, transcripts, and playback; provider continuity retains its own epoch. Playback and exact-speech state are closed unions rather than parallel booleans.
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e250]:
                      - text: "Discord startup keeps its existing outer lazy boundary, while STT/TTS no longer loads realtime provider and consult machinery:"
                      - code [ref=f9e251]: voice-session.ts
                      - text: dynamically imports
                      - code [ref=f9e252]: realtime-session.runtime.ts
                      - text: only for agent-proxy and bidi modes. Generic policy text and the exact-speech scraper remain byte-identical and are explicitly marked for a separate owner-reviewed realtime-voice follow-up.
                    - heading "User Impact" [level=2] [ref=f9e253]
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e254]: Discord voice leave, follow, capture, and realtime operations are last-event-wins. Stale async work can no longer reconnect, subscribe, transcribe, play, publish readiness, or deliver provider completions after its voice generation has closed.
                    - paragraph [ref=f9e255]: There is no configuration or Plugin SDK change. Existing voice instructions, consult/control tool schemas, transcript-source behavior, and STT/TTS behavior are preserved.
