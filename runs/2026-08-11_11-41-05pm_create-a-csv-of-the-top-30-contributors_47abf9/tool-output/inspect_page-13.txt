              - text: "asks a deceptively simple question:"
              - strong [ref=f12e199]: what should income-tax rates be if government spending claims have to prove themselves first?
            - paragraph [ref=f12e200]: Instead of beginning with a desired rate or headline savings number, TAXLANE examines fifteen public-purpose and accounting tracks through one evidence spine. It tests the fiscal object, source currency, net cost, overlap, beneficiary and service-continuity effects, implementation burden, and distribution boundary before admitting a spending reduction. Payment integrity stays non-additive, interest follows the debt path, dedicated old-age solvency stays separate, and revenue finances only the scoped remainder.

              - strong [ref=f12e204]: 21/23/33/35/43/46/48
              - text: "as its preferred analytical marginal bracket schedule, with distinct behavior-contingency and severe-stress rails. Zero is a result: unsupported savings are excluded rather than guessed."
