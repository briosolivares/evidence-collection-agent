# YC Winter 2024 AI-Focused Companies — Selection Summary

## Selected 5 companies (all tagged "Artificial Intelligence" / "AI" and batch = Winter 2024 on the YC Startup Directory)

1. **Retell AI** — Supercharge Your Contact Center Operations with AI Phone Agents
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retell-ai
   Founders: Bing Wu, Todd Li, Evie Wang, Weijia Yu, Zexia Zhang

2. **Greptile** — AI code review agent with complete context of your codebase
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greptile
   Founders: Daksh Gupta, Soohoon Choi, Vaishant Kameswaran

3. **Zep AI** — Agent Context Is Hard. We Fixed It. (memory layer for LLM agents)
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zep-ai
   Founders: Daniel Chalef

4. **Artisan** — The AI BDR for enterprise revenue teams, plus a full AI sales team
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/artisan
   Founders: Jaspar Carmichael-Jack

5. **Decipher AI** — QA agents that write tests 10x faster with zero maintenance
   https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/decipher-ai
   Founders: Michael Rosenfield, Rohan Das

Total founders across all 5 companies: **12**

## Methodology
- Used the YC Startup Directory (ycombinator.com/companies) filtered to Batch = Winter 2024 and searched/filtered for AI-related companies.
- Verified each selected company's page individually to confirm the "Winter 2024" batch tag and an "Artificial Intelligence"/"AI" industry tag, and to extract the full "Active Founders" list with names, roles, bios, and LinkedIn URLs.
- One candidate (CodeAnt AI) was excluded after verification showed its current YC industry tag had shifted to "Cybersecurity" rather than AI.

## Deliverables
- `artifacts/yc_w24_ai_founders_outreach.csv` — CSV with columns founder_name, linkedin_url, cold_outreach_email; one row per founder (12 rows), each email personalized to the founder's background/role and company, and each asking for a 15-minute call.
- `artifacts/evidence_retell_ai.png`, `artifacts/evidence_greptile.png`, `artifacts/evidence_zep_ai.png`, `artifacts/evidence_artisan.png`, `artifacts/evidence_decipher_ai.png` — full-page screenshots of each company's YC profile page as source evidence.
